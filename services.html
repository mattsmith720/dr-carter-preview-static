Services

Assessment and management

Time to talk through a concern, examine you if needed, and agree what happens next.

01

General consultations

Assessment and management of general health concerns in an unhurried appointment. You should leave knowing what is being considered and how to seek help if things change.

02

Referrals and care coordination

Assessment of when another clinician should be involved, discussion with you first, and correspondence so care stays joined up.

03

Chronic conditions

Ongoing assessment and management of long-term health conditions, including reviews of medicines and care plans where they are appropriate.

04

Men’s and women’s health

Assessment and management of common men’s and women’s health concerns across adult life, discussed in plain language.

05

Skin checks

Clinical assessment of moles and skin changes. Further management or referral is discussed if needed.

06

Preventative care

Health checks, screening discussions and lifestyle advice aimed at reducing risk — without pressure to have tests you do not need.

The areas listed here are a starting point and will be matched to Dr Carter’s actual scope of practice once confirmed. This page does not list procedures beyond those headings. Results differ between people.

If you have severe or worsening symptoms, call 000 or go to an emergency department.
