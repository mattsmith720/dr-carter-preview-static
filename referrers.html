Referrers

Referring to Dr Carter

A clear path for general practitioners in Brisbane.

Thank you for considering a referral. Correspondence back to the referring doctor is part of the visit when that is appropriate.

How to refer

Until telephone, fax and secure messaging details are published, please use the contact form to request the current referral path. Tick that this is a referral. Do not attach clinical records to a public web form.

A standard GP referral letter is appreciated for a first appointment and may be required for a Medicare rebate. Once rooms details are confirmed, referrals can be sent by the path the practice nominates — typically telephone, fax or secure messaging.

What to include

  • Patient identifying details and contact numbers
  • The clinical question you would like addressed
  • Relevant history, medicines and allergies
  • Key investigations already done
  • Whether the matter is routine or time-sensitive

Urgent matters

Do not use this website for urgent referrals. Direct the patient to an emergency department or call 000 as clinically indicated.

A note on public hospitals

This is a private practice website. It does not take public-hospital referrals and does not represent Queensland Health, Metro North Health or Redcliffe Hospital.
