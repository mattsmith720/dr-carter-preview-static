About

Dr Murray Carter

A registered medical practitioner in Redcliffe, Queensland.

Dr Murray Carter is a registered medical practitioner on the Redcliffe Peninsula, in Greater Brisbane. He provides consultations for patients and works with referring general practitioners as part of coordinated care.

A first visit usually covers your history, an examination if needed, and a plan you have both agreed on. Outcomes vary. No website can say what will happen in your case.

Approach

Dr Carter aims to explain findings in plain language and to share decisions with you. He will say if a test or procedure may not help, and what the main uncertainties are.

Where another clinician needs to be involved, that is discussed with you first.

Registration

Qualifications and Ahpra registration will be shown on this page once they have been confirmed against the public Register of Practitioners.

Anyone may check the register at ahpra.gov.au.

Languages

Consultations are conducted in English.
