Privacy

How we handle information

A concise draft policy under the Australian Privacy Principles. Rooms contact details will be added once confirmed.

Dr Murray Carter is the APP entity for this private practice in Brisbane, Queensland. This policy describes how personal information is collected, used and held. It is a draft until a practice postal address and telephone number are published.

What we collect

We collect information you give us (for example your name, telephone, email, whether a GP has referred you, and the type of appointment you are seeking). During a consultation we also collect health information that is needed to assess and manage your care. We do not ask you to send clinical information through a public web form.

Why we collect it

We collect personal and health information to respond to enquiries, arrange appointments, provide medical care, correspond with referring doctors and other clinicians involved in your care, meet legal and professional obligations, and manage the practice. If you do not provide information we reasonably need, we may not be able to see you or to reply.

Use and disclosure

We use information for those purposes. We may disclose it to other health practitioners involved in your care, to Medicare or insurers where a rebate or claim requires it, to our professional advisers, and otherwise as Australian law requires or authorises. We do not sell personal information. We do not routinely disclose information overseas.

Storage and security

Information is held in paper and electronic records used by the practice. We take reasonable steps to protect it from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Web-form requests on this preview are not transmitted to a server until a practice email is connected.

Access and correction

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading (APPs 12 and 13). We may need to verify your identity. In a small number of cases we may refuse access where the Privacy Act allows, and we will explain why.

Complaints

If you are concerned about how we have handled your information, please contact the practice using the contact form so we can try to put it right. You may also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

This website

This site uses no marketing cookies and no third-party analytics in this preview. Links to Ahpra and the OAIC leave our site.

This is a draft policy for a practice website. It is not legal advice. It will be reviewed before the site goes live, once rooms details and record-keeping arrangements are confirmed.
