Patients

Coming in

How to request a time, what to have with you, and when not to wait.

Appointments

Request a time through the contact form. The practice will reply with rooms details and available appointments. Please do not attend until a time and address have been confirmed with you.

If you cannot keep an appointment, let the rooms know as soon as you can so the time can be offered to someone else.

What to bring

  • Your Medicare card, and any concession card
  • A current list of medicines and allergies
  • Recent results or letters, if you have them
  • A referral from your GP, if you have one
  • Glasses, or a short written list of questions

Medicare and referrals

Medicare rebates may apply, depending on the service and on whether a valid referral is needed. The practice will tell you if a GP referral is required for your appointment.

Fees are available on request. A written estimate is provided before a procedure. See Fees.

After hours

This website is not monitored for urgent problems. If you need care and the rooms are closed, go to an emergency department or call 000.

For a medical emergency, call 000 or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not use this website for urgent care.
