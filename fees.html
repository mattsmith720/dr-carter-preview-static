Fees

Available on request

Clear figures, given before you decide. Not a teaser list.

Fees for consultations and procedures are available on request from the practice. Ask when you book, or use the contact form.

A written estimate will be provided before a procedure so you can consider the cost in advance.

Medicare

Medicare rebates may apply to some services, depending on the item, on whether a valid referral is required, and on your circumstances. Any gap is not published here, because it can vary.

What this page does not do

This page does not list bulk-billing rules, gap amounts or a fee schedule. Those will be added only if Dr Carter confirms complete, current figures. A published price, if shown later, will be the total price for that service.

Payment arrangements will be confirmed when you book.
